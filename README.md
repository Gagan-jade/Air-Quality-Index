
# 🌫️ Air Quality Impact Predictor for Bangalore 🏭

This project is a web-based tool that predicts the future **Air Quality Index (AQI)** of specific locations in **Bangalore** over the next **5–10 years**, based on the planned placement of new industries.

It aims to assist **urban planners**, **policy makers**, and **environmental analysts** by estimating how proposed industrial development may affect air quality in surrounding areas, helping guide smarter and more sustainable city planning decisions.

---

## 🚀 Features

- 🔮 Predicts AQI for a specific location over a 5–10 year period  
- 🏭 Takes industry type and location into account  
- 📍 Highlights areas at environmental risk before planning approval  
- 📊 Machine learning-powered predictions based on historical pollution data  
- 🧠 Encourages data-driven policy and sustainable urban growth
